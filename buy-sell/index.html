Buy & sell

Thinking of selling? Ready to buy?

Because Gaby is a licensed broker, your property manager can also be your listing agent, or your buyer’s agent.

Owners: sell with confidence

Ready to sell a managed property? Gaby can get it under contract and list it, coordinating with your renter and the existing lease so the transition is smooth.

  • Buyer’s agent representation
  • Plan your move around your lease end
  • No obligation to start the conversation

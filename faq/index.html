FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything owners, renters and contractors ask us most.

How do I become a client?

Create an owner account in the portal, review and e-sign the management agreement online, then add your property. The whole process takes a few minutes.

What happens when my renter reports a problem?

The renter submits the issue with photos. You and the Anchor & Atlas team are emailed instantly. A contractor provides an estimate, you approve it, we offer dates, and the renter confirms one. Everyone receives an email at each step.

Will my renter see repair costs?

No. Estimates, quotes and invoices are visible only to the owner and the management team. Renters see the issue status and the dates offered.

Can small repairs be approved automatically?

Yes. You can set a spending limit. Repairs below it proceed without waiting for you, and you are still notified and receive every receipt.

How do I pay for management and repairs?

Through the portal, using secure online payments powered by Stripe.

Who is notified about each repair?

Owner, management team, renter and contractor are all emailed at the milestones that concern them. Cost details go only to the owner and the management team.

Do contractors get a login?

Yes. Contractors see their assigned jobs with the renter’s photos, upload estimates and share availability.

Can Anchor & Atlas help me sell my property?

Yes. Gaby Wolfe is a licensed broker and can list your property for sale. She can also act as a buyer’s agent for renters who are ready to buy.

Which areas do you serve?

San Diego and surrounding neighborhoods. See our neighborhoods page, or ask us about a specific area.

Do you work with military families?

Yes. Military housing is one of our specialties. We help service members find rentals that fit their orders and housing allowance, and we help owners on orders keep and rent their San Diego homes. See our Military Housing page.

Can a service member end a lease early for orders?

Federal law (the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act) gives eligible service members certain rights to end a lease for deployment or a permanent change of station, with written notice and a copy of orders. The rules have conditions, so check Military OneSource or a legal assistance office for your situation. This is general information, not legal advice.

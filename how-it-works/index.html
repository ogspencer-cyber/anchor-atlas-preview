How it works

One workflow. Three people. Zero phone tag.

Here is exactly what happens, and who is told, from the moment an owner signs up to the moment a repair is finished.

Step one

Becoming a client takes minutes.

1

Visit & create an account

Owners register with their name, email and property details.

2

E-sign the agreement

Management agreement and NDA, signed online and stored securely.

3

Add the property & renter

Invite the renter so they get their own login automatically.

4

Pay & relax

The monthly management rate and any repairs are paid through the portal.

Step two

The repair loop, and who sees what.

Every event sends emails to exactly the right people. Renters never see quotes or costs.

1

Renter reports a problem

Photos, category and urgency attached.

OwnerManagerRenter
✉ Confirmation to renter; alert to owner & team
2

Contractor is assigned

Request and photos are forwarded.

ManagerContractor
✉ Job details only
3

Estimate is entered

By the contractor or our team.

OwnerManager
✉ Costs visible to owner & team only
4

Owner approves

One tap, or auto-approved under the limit.

OwnerManagerContractor
✉ Contractor told to proceed (no price shown to renter)
5

Dates are offered

Manager posts contractor availability.

RenterOwnerManager
✉ Renter sees dates, never costs
6

Renter confirms a date

Closes the scheduling loop.

OwnerManagerRenterContractor
✉ Everyone gets who, what and when
7

Work completed & invoiced

Invoice paid securely in the portal.

OwnerManager
✉ Receipt to owner; renter gets a completion notice

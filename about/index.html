Founder & broker

Her first deal was $3 million. Her calm, organized approach is still how she works.

Gaby Wolfe began her career in 2004 on a high-performing Prudential team in La Jolla and closed a $3 million property as her very first transaction. More than two decades later, she is still guiding clients through major purchases, most recently a $1.7 million home in La Jolla.

She earned her California broker license in 2005, then co-founded a group focused on investment property sales. There she built the property management side from the ground up, creating systems so owners get strong transaction support and ongoing care from one team.

“Organization is the foundation of every successful real estate relationship.”

As a certified Project Management Professional (PMP), Gaby brings that discipline to every owner’s account: clear processes, steady communication and no surprises.

Military housing

Housing for those who serve San Diego.

Orders arrive on their own schedule. We build the housing plan around it, for service members renting here and for owners who have PCS’d away.

Service members renting in San Diego

Moving on a tight timeline? Tell us your branch, base and report date and we will focus on homes that fit your orders and housing allowance.

  • Search shaped around your report date
  • Straight answers on lease terms before you sign
  • Online portal for repairs and updates, even mid-deployment

Owners on military orders

Orders to a new duty station but you want to keep your San Diego home? We manage it while you are away, with a full owner portal you can use from anywhere.

  • Rent your home with a licensed broker’s guidance
  • Approve repairs from your phone, any time zone
  • Sell later with the same trusted team if plans change
Built around PCS timing

Moving soon? Start here.

A permanent change of station comes with deadlines: report dates, household goods pickups, lease end dates. We plan backwards from your orders so housing is one less thing to worry about.

  • Tell us your branch, base and report date
  • We line up options that fit your timing and budget
  • Sign your lease online, from wherever you are
  • Get keys, then handle repair requests through your portal
Near the installations

Neighborhoods close to where you serve.

Commutes matter. These are some of the San Diego areas service members commonly look at, depending on their duty station.

Coronado & Point Loma

Close to Naval Air Station North Island and the Point Loma naval installations.

Chula Vista & National City

South Bay options near Naval Base San Diego.

Mira Mesa & Scripps Ranch

North side of the city, near MCAS Miramar.

Clairemont & Kearny Mesa

Central location with freeway access to several bases.

Mission Valley & Hillcrest

Central, walkable neighborhoods close to Balboa Park and the medical center.

Oceanside & Carlsbad

North coastal communities for those stationed at or near Camp Pendleton.

Commute times vary with traffic and gate access. We will help you check your own route.
